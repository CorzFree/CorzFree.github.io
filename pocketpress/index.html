Good to know

PocketPress questions, answered.

Can I compress a photo to exactly 100 KB or 200 KB?

You can set a target size. The result depends on the image and your settings, so always check the exported file. Very small limits can reduce detail. Compression is not lossless.

Does photo compression work offline?

Yes, processing of local photos happens on your Android device. PocketPress does not upload selected photos to a developer server. A cloud-stored photo may need to download first. Advertising and privacy-message services use an internet connection.

Which image format should I choose?

PocketPress exports JPEG and WebP. Choose the format accepted by your destination. If a form specifically asks for JPG or JPEG, export JPEG and check any dimension requirements as well as its size limit.

Will my original photos be replaced?

No. PocketPress saves new copies. You can clear recent outputs inside the app; copies already saved to your gallery or shared elsewhere must be deleted in those locations.

Do I need to watch an ad to use the tools?

No. A small banner appears on the results screen. Photo tools do not require a rewarded ad, and this version has no full-screen ads or ad-gated features.
