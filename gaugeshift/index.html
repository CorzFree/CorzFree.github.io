The answer, with receipts

No mystery. Just the math your stitches are already doing.

1

Project the real size

A 40-inch size at 20 stitches becomes 44.4 inches when your swatch measures 18 stitches over the same width.

40 × 20 ÷ 18 = 44.4
2

Compare every size

We project each published finished measurement at your gauge, then show the closest match to your body plus intended ease.

closest to body × (1 + ease)
3

Respect the repeat

Converted stitch counts are rounded to whole pattern repeats plus fixed edge stitches—and the width tradeoff stays visible.

Know the boundary

GaugeShift helps you plan.
It does not rewrite a pattern.

Good fit

Simple garments, straight sections, size comparison, yarn substitution planning, and stitch-repeat rounding.

Pause & check

Raglans, circular yokes, sleeve caps, neck shaping, picked-up bands, cables, lace, and colorwork.

Always do

Swatch in the same method, wash and block it, judge the fabric, and measure as you knit.
