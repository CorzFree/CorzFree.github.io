Effective date: 7 September 2026

The game saves your progress on your device. It has no developer-operated account or cloud-save service. Advertising services process certain device and usage data. This policy explains those differences and your choices.

1. What this policy covers

This policy covers Aster Loop, its support correspondence and these game support pages. Aster Loop is a single-player arcade game. You do not need to create an account, and the game has no developer-operated online leaderboard, gameplay analytics server or in-app purchases.

2. Game information stored locally

The app saves scores, earned stardust, unlocked cosmetics, milestone progress, daily results and preferences using Android app storage. These saved records are not uploaded to a server operated by Corzfree. The app disables Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of its data.

A live run is held in memory and is not restored after the process ends. Banked progress is saved at relevant game transitions; unsaved changes can be lost if the app stops unexpectedly.

3. Advertising and Google's services

The game integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Ads help fund the game. Rewarded ads are offered for the in-game benefit described before you choose to watch. Interstitial ads may appear at eligible breaks between completed runs. Playing the game does not require choosing a rewarded offer.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, the SDK processes network location signals, ad or app interaction records, technical diagnostics, and advertising or other device/account identifiers. Uses include ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention; data sent by this SDK is encrypted in transit. Read Google's SDK disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps.

Privacy messages and choices vary by region and applicable requirements. Where available, use the game's Settings → Privacy choices to reopen the supported consent options. A choice about personalized ads does not necessarily stop all advertising-related data processing. Android's advertising ID controls provide additional choices. Ads may be unavailable when consent or connectivity requirements cannot be met; ordinary gameplay remains available.

Corzfree receives advertising reports through AdMob. Google and advertising participants handle data under their applicable policies and may process it in countries other than yours. The game does not send its local score or wallet records to a Corzfree backend for advertising.

4. Support emails and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. The support address uses Gmail. We use correspondence to answer your request, troubleshoot issues and handle privacy requests; we do not add you to a marketing list merely because you contact support. Correspondence is retained as needed to resolve and document the request or meet applicable obligations. You may request deletion of correspondence we control using the contact address below.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Requests to the hosting service can disclose connection details such as an IP address and browser information to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. These game pages contain no developer-added analytics, advertising tags, contact-form processor or third-party font loader. Opening a linked external website is governed by that provider's practices.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you clear app storage or uninstall the app. To remove it, open Android Settings → Apps → Aster Loop → Storage → Clear storage; labels vary by device. This removes saved progress, cosmetics and preferences and cannot be undone through the game. There is no remote game account to delete and no in-game reset button in this version.

Clearing the app does not erase data already held by Google, GitHub or your email provider. Those providers describe their retention periods and controls in their linked policies. For information or support emails controlled by Corzfree, email free4564567@gmail.com with the subject “Aster Loop Privacy Request” and describe what you want to access, correct or delete. We may request information needed to verify the request and will respond as required by applicable law. We cannot promise to delete records that a separate provider controls.

6. Audience and permissions

Aster Loop is designed for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. This intended audience does not determine any individual user's legal age of consent. A parent or guardian who believes a child provided personal information directly to Corzfree can contact us so we can review the request and remove information under our control where appropriate.

The game does not request your name, password, precise GPS position, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or payment details. Advertising services may process device or account identifiers as explained above.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy regulator. Contact Corzfree about information we control; use Google's linked resources for Google-held advertising information. Withdrawing consent does not change the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Where applicable, support processing is based on providing the service you request and our legitimate interest in answering inquiries and maintaining the game. Consent is sought for advertising processing where required; providers explain their own purposes and legal bases in their policies.

8. Security and policy updates

The game uses Android's app-storage protections. No storage or transmission system can be guaranteed completely secure. If our practices change, we will update this page and its effective date and provide additional notice where required.
